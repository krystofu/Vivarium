VIVARIUM LUSTREHEAT CHARACTER ART STYLE DNA v1.3
Canonical Hybrid Glamour-Illustration Build
Status: Production Target

==================================================
CORE STYLE EQUATION
==================================================

LUSTREHEAT v1.3 = LUXURY GLAMOUR ILLUSTRATION + CINEMATIC REALISM + CONTROLLED ANIME-EDITORIAL DESIGN

Target visual balance:

55% high-end glamour illustration
30% cinematic realism
15% anime/editorial stylization

The final image should resist immediate classification as a photograph, painting, or premium character render.

That ambiguity is intentional.

The character should feel physically believable, materially rich, and gorgeously illuminated, while still carrying the authored perfection, expressive facial design, painterly finish, and visual idealization of premium character art.

Do not force obvious anime geometry.
Do not force obvious photorealism.
Do not create a realistic woman and merely apply a stylized filter afterward.

The correct result feels like a beautiful real moment remembered through an idealized painterly lens.


==================================================
PRIMARY CREATIVE DIRECTIVE
==================================================

Create premium vertical 2:3 character artwork featuring an unmistakably adult woman with:

- a highly readable and memorable face
- a decisive feminine silhouette
- active awareness of the viewer
- a clear relationship beat
- character-native sensuality
- tactile clothing and materials
- a genuinely lived-in setting
- visible kinetic evidence
- cinematic physical light
- glossy painterly polish
- elegant controlled idealization

The image must feel alive.

The character should never read as a mannequin waiting for a camera.

She was already doing something.

The viewer entered her space.

She noticed.

Her expression, posture, gaze, or gesture changed because of that interruption.


==================================================
THE LUSTREHEAT v1.3 LOOK
==================================================

Lustreheat v1.3 is not primarily anime.

It is a hybrid premium visual language built from:

- high-end glamour illustration
- golden-hour cinematic realism
- premium game-key-art polish
- fashion-editorial facial beauty
- subtly stylized character anatomy
- glossy illustrated eyes
- painterly lips
- flowing designed hair
- tactile materials
- rich environmental storytelling
- warm atmospheric depth
- controlled idealization

The ideal image should feel:

luxurious
warm
sensual
cinematic
painterly
polished
emotionally immediate
physically convincing
slightly unreal in the best way


==================================================
CHARACTER / WORLD REALISM SPLIT
==================================================

The character and environment should not use identical realism levels.

CHARACTER:
approximately 55% glamour illustration
30% cinematic realism
15% anime/editorial stylization

WORLD:
approximately 35% painterly illustration
65% physical realism

The woman may be slightly more idealized than the room around her.

Keep the room, reflections, fabrics, wood, metal, denim, glass, clutter, gravity, light falloff, and spatial depth physically convincing.

Keep the woman smoother, cleaner, more authored, more luminous, and more compositionally perfect.

Stylize the woman.
Realize the world.
Unify both with one painterly finish.


==================================================
IDENTITY LOCK
==================================================

When reference images are supplied, treat them as an appearance lock, not loose inspiration.

Preserve:

- recognizable facial structure
- eye shape and color
- brow shape
- nose
- mouth
- skin tone
- hairline
- hairstyle
- hair color
- body type
- height impression
- tattoos
- piercings
- scars
- makeup style
- permanent accessories
- other identity anchors

Do not replace the supplied woman with a generic anime beauty, generic glamour model, or unrelated idealized face.

Beauty may be polished.

Identity must survive.


==================================================
FACE PRIORITY
==================================================

The face is the primary attraction anchor.

It should carry the strongest fusion of realism and illustration.

Use:

- highly readable expressive eyes
- luminous irises
- crisp catchlights
- elegant lashes
- softly sculpted cheeks
- refined but believable nose modeling
- plush painterly lips
- subtle natural asymmetry
- warm reflected light
- controlled facial idealization
- emotionally specific mouth shape
- eye contact that feels directed toward a person

The eyes may be slightly more visually dominant than strict photographic realism, but they should not become overtly anime-sized.

The nose should remain elegant and physically believable.

The lips should feel painted and intentionally shaped rather than mechanically photoreal.

The face should feel glamorous and authored without becoming plastic.

Avoid:

- generic anime-face replacement
- blank beauty-campaign expression
- excessive facial microtexture
- hyper-sharp pores
- doll-like emptiness
- obvious CGI facial smoothness
- perfectly symmetrical facial design


==================================================
SKIN RENDERING
==================================================

Skin should feel smooth, warm, luminous, and painterly.

Use:

- broad warm gradients
- gentle blush
- creamy reflected highlights
- soft shadow transitions
- restrained specular accents
- warm subsurface glow
- selective glossy polish
- cinematic falloff

Do not render every body curve with equal highlight intensity.

Do not turn every anatomical surface into a glossy glamour-photo contour.

Concentrate the most precise rendering around:

1. face
2. eyes
3. lips
4. hairline
5. hands
6. primary silhouette transitions

Let secondary anatomy resolve through broader softer gradients.

The skin should feel idealized and alive, not pore-perfect and not airbrushed into plastic.


==================================================
BODY AND SILHOUETTE
==================================================

Preserve the character's established adult body proportions.

Choose one dominant silhouette idea.

Examples:

- narrow waist + feminine hips + long legs
- petite frame + curvy lower-body contrast
- slim athletic torso + strong legs
- tall elegant line + restrained curves
- soft compact frame + pronounced waist-to-hip contrast

Do not maximize every feature simultaneously.

A strong Lustreheat silhouette is memorable because one or two proportion ideas dominate.

The body should feel:

attractive
coherent
adult
physically grounded
stylized with restraint
responsive to posture and gravity

Keep shoulders, waist, hips, knees, hands, ankles, and limb transitions anatomically believable.

Do not flatten the body into anime abstraction.

Do not inflate it into hyper-pin-up anatomy either.


==================================================
HAIR
==================================================

Hair should combine designed flow with believable strand detail.

First establish:

- large intentional hair masses
- readable silhouette
- character-specific shape
- natural volume
- clear movement direction

Then add selective loose strands around:

- face
- shoulders
- rim light
- movement edges

Do not render every hair strand with equal photographic sharpness.

Hair should feel:

soft
luminous
slightly messy
touchable
painterly
cinematically backlit

The best result feels hyper-detailed from a distance but still clearly designed on close inspection.


==================================================
VIEWER RELATIONSHIP RULE
==================================================

The camera represents another person physically present in the scene.

The woman is not posing into empty space.

She has seen the viewer and reacted.

The image should communicate three things immediately:

1. She saw me.
2. She reacted to me.
3. What happens next depends on how I respond.

The relationship beat may be:

- amused interruption
- playful challenge
- affectionate recognition
- teasing invitation
- possessive attention
- curious hesitation
- warm familiarity
- quiet confidence
- sudden surprise
- knowing flirtation

The reaction should fit the character.

Do not paste the same flirtatious expression onto every woman.


==================================================
CANDID MOMENT RULE
==================================================

The strongest Lustreheat images feel captured approximately 0.4 to 0.8 seconds after something changed.

Examples:

- she was fixing her hair and noticed the viewer
- she had just turned toward a sound
- she was adjusting jewelry
- she was pulling on a jacket
- she had just sat down
- she had just laughed
- she was reaching for something
- she had just opened a drawer
- she was stepping through a doorway
- she was turning away but looked back
- she had just caught the viewer staring

The pose should exist because of the action.

The action should not exist merely to justify the pose.


==================================================
THREE-HEAT-CUE RULE
==================================================

Use approximately three primary attraction cues at once.

Recommended categories:

1. EXPRESSION
2. GESTURE
3. SILHOUETTE

Example:

Expression:
bright crooked smile with direct playful eye contact

Gesture:
one hand gathering her hair while the other touches a necklace

Silhouette:
relaxed asymmetric weight shift emphasizing her established waist-and-hip shape

Additional sensual cues should remain secondary.

Do not stack every possible seductive signal into one image.

Restraint makes the important cues hit harder.

==================================================

The pose must function as a verb.

Prefer:

Use:

- natural weight shift

- body language that reveals personality

Avoid:

- centered idle standing
- fashion-catalogue posing
- perfect pin-up symmetry
- mannequin stillness
- anatomically disconnected glamour poses


==================================================
KINETIC EVIDENCE
==================================================

Show evidence of what happened moments before the frame.

Examples:

- loose hair still settling
- necklace chain still moving
- shirt hem displaced
- curtain lifting in window air
- bed covers recently disturbed
- drawer left open
- makeup brush out of place
- jacket slipping from a chair
- swinging earring
- moving reflection
- fabric caught mid-fall
- shifting light
- dust in sunlight
- displaced jewelry
- drink surface still moving

Kinetic evidence should support the action, not become visual clutter.


==================================================
FASHION
==================================================

Fashion should feel native to the character.

Use one hero fashion idea and two or three supporting details.

Clothing should reveal:

- taste
- subculture
- occupation
- mood
- environment
- social identity
- current intention

Render materials distinctly:

- denim
- cotton
- ribbed knit
- leather
- satin
- lace
- jewelry
- metal
- glass
- cosmetics
- boots
- tights
- mesh
- wool
- silk

Clothing should show:

- natural folds
- slight compression
- imperfect hems
- believable gravity
- uneven tension
- lived-in wear
- tactile surface variation

Do not let wardrobe feel like random costume assembly.


==================================================
LIGHTING
==================================================

Light the face first.

Preferred Lustreheat lighting:

- warm late-afternoon or golden-hour key light
- soft neutral or slightly cool room fill
- delicate warm rim light
- rich warm shadow structure
- cinematic falloff
- controlled bloom
- luminous eye catchlights
- painterly reflected light

Let sunlight create broad golden shapes across:

face
hair
shoulders
waist
legs
furniture
room surfaces

Keep highlights creamy and warm rather than metallic white.

Allow parts of the environment to fall into shadow.

Avoid pristine beauty-studio illumination.

The light should feel intimate, physical, warm, and slightly nostalgic.


==================================================
ENVIRONMENT
==================================================

The setting should feel genuinely inhabited.

A Lustreheat room should tell us something about the character before she says a word.

Use concrete personal evidence such as:

- rumpled bedding
- open drawer
- makeup
- hairbrush
- jewelry
- discarded clothing
- books
- boots
- cosmetics
- mirror
- vanity
- plants
- photos without readable text
- hobby objects
- work items
- personal clutter
- half-used objects
- warm wooden furniture
- curtains
- lamps
- clothing draped over furniture

The environment should not feel staged for a photoshoot.

It should feel like her space.


==================================================
NO GENERATED WRITING
==================================================

Do not place readable writing anywhere in the image.

No:

- slogans
- captions
- handwritten notes
- posters with legible text
- product labels
- logos
- signatures
- watermarks
- decorative typography
- visible interface text

Environmental decoration should remain purely visual.

If papers, posters, books, packaging, photos, or screens appear, any markings should be abstract, illegible, or non-textual.


==================================================
COMPOSITION
==================================================

Use an exact vertical 2:3 composition.

Default framing:
three-quarter to nearly full body

Place:

- face in upper third
- primary expression at thumbnail-readable scale
- dominant gesture in upper or middle frame
- body silhouette through middle
- supporting environment around her

Use:

- slight perspective
- natural asymmetry
- environmental overlap
- depth layers
- mild foreground cropping
- off-center framing when useful

Avoid perfect centered symmetry.

Keep the full silhouette readable when full-body framing is requested.

For LoreBary marketplace use, protect important information from the lower interface region whenever applicable.


==================================================
DETAIL HIERARCHY
==================================================

Highest-detail zones:

1. eyes
2. mouth
3. face
4. hair framing the face
5. hands
6. hero clothing details
7. signature jewelry or prop

Medium detail:

- torso
- waist
- hips
- main clothing materials
- nearby furniture
- major environmental objects

Lower detail:

- secondary anatomy
- distant room clutter
- peripheral background
- nonessential textures

The eye should know exactly where to look.


==================================================
COLOR
==================================================

Use a limited intentional palette.

Lustreheat favors:

- warm amber
- cream
- pale lavender
- sun-washed denim
- rich brown
- muted rose
- deep charcoal
- soft gold
- warm skin tones
- small accent colors such as turquoise, crimson, silver, or emerald

Do not make every object equally saturated.

Use color to support the face, silhouette, and atmosphere.

==================================================

The finished image should feel like:

a premium character illustration
painted with cinematic physical light
using high-end glamour rendering
with subtle anime-editorial idealization
inside a believable lived-in world

The woman should feel:

beautiful
distinctive
warm
aware
physically present
slightly idealized
emotionally legible
character-specific
viewer-responsive

The image should carry the seductive polish of luxury glamour art without collapsing into an ordinary glamour photograph.

It should carry the authored beauty of anime/editorial character art without looking overtly anime.

It should carry the material richness of realism without becoming sterile CGI.

The target is the unstable, beautiful middle ground where the viewer can no longer immediately decide:

photograph?
painting?
character render?

That uncertainty is part of Lustreheat.
