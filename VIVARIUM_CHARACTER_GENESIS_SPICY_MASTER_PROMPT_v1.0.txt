VIVARIUM CHARACTER GENESIS SPICY MASTER PROMPT v1.0
Canonical New-Character Identity + High-Heat Reference Image System

PURPOSE

Use this master ONLY when creating a brand-new, unmistakably adult character who does not yet have a trusted visual reference.

GENESIS SPICY has two jobs at once:

1. Establish a precise, reusable visual identity that later models can reproduce.
2. Make the first canonical image sexually charged enough that it already feels like a private, tempting moment rather than a sterile reference sheet.

The reference image must remain clean enough to reveal the character's true face, body proportions, hair, skin, and permanent anchors, but the erotic pressure should begin at the level of a very daring lingerie / mid-change / private-room image rather than ordinary glamour photography.

CORE PRINCIPLE

GENESIS SPICY ESTABLISHES TRUTH UNDER PRESSURE.

Do not choose between identity and eroticism.
Build the eroticism out of the same physical facts that reveal identity.

The character should be caught in the loaded middle of doing something: changing, dressing, undressing, adjusting clothing, drying off, leaning for a drink, stepping out of a shower, fastening jewelry, pulling on a stocking, cooling off by a window, sitting on the edge of a bed, or another character-native private action.

The image should feel as though the shutter fired approximately 0.4 seconds after the most charged part of that action became visible.

SPICE FLOOR

The default GENESIS SPICY result should be at least as erotically charged as:
- intimate private setting
- revealing lingerie, sleepwear, swimwear, thin lounge clothing, or another strongly body-readable outfit
- one unresolved garment or body-adjacent state
- one deliberate non-correction
- strong viewer awareness
- a body twist, lean, compression, reach, or weight shift caused by a real action
- one second-glance reward
- tactile material behavior
- enough skin and silhouette information to make the body immediately legible

A simple fitted dress, ordinary crop top, generic pin-up pose, or neutral bikini portrait is BELOW the default spice floor unless the staging itself creates equivalent erotic pressure.

Do not repeatedly use the words sexy, seductive, provocative, erotic, horny, or tempting as substitutes for image mechanics.
Describe the physical circumstances that make the image feel that way.

THE CAUGHT-IN-THE-ACT RULE

Do not begin from a pose.
Begin from an activity.

She was already doing:
[PRIOR ACTION]

The frame catches the action before it returns to neutral.

Examples of useful mechanics:
- shirt halfway off one shoulder
- one sleeve still trapped around an arm
- bra strap displaced by movement
- robe hanging from the elbows
- waistband being adjusted rather than simply displayed
- stockings only partly pulled into place
- damp fabric still clinging after water
- dress zipper partly lowered while changing
- camisole twisted from turning
- skirt or shorts shifted slightly from sitting or reaching
- hair still moving from a turn
- one shoe already kicked off

The moment should look temporally unstable. Something is still happening.

VIEWER HISTORY

The photographer has already been present long enough that:
[VIEWER HISTORY]

Avoid first-contact surprise as the default.

Do not write dialogue into the image. Stage that realization physically.

CLOTHING STATE OVER CLOTHING CATEGORY

Do not ask only what she is wearing.
Ask what the garment is doing RIGHT NOW.

Material behavior should be physically believable:
- lace stretches and compresses
- thin straps shift
- wet cotton darkens and clings
- silk slips
- satin catches narrow highlights
- jersey bunches and pulls
- denim creases
- fine knit follows body tension
- linen grows heavier and less crisp with moisture

Default HARD TEASE clothing can include daring lingerie, high-cut briefs or thong-backed lingerie, soft-cup bras, bralettes, bodysuits, slips, tiny lounge shorts, fitted camisoles, swimwear, open shirts, robes, stockings, or combinations that keep the body readable.

SITE LANE

HARD TEASE, DEFAULT:
Push to the strongest non-explicit result the platform is likely to allow. Daring lingerie, thong-level rear coverage, deep but covered cleavage, open shirts, slipping straps, mid-change states, wet or clinging materials, high-cut silhouettes, and strongly suggestive undressing mechanics are all valid.

UNCENSORED, WHERE SUPPORTED:
The same scene logic may use more revealing adult nudity or exposure, but nudity must still arise from the action, preserve the character's agency, and remain useful as identity reference. Do not let raw exposure replace expression, physics, or character specificity.

SECOND-GLANCE REWARD

Include exactly one detail that becomes more provocative after a second look.

Examples:
- the viewer realizes one clasp is open
- a shirt is not merely loose but actually sliding off
- a hand is still hooked at a waistband
- damp fabric has become more body-readable
- a back turn reveals more than the first glance suggests
- a robe is being held only by one hand

Do not stack five reveals.
One coherent discovery is stronger.

CAMERA RELATIONSHIP

[CAMERA RELATIONSHIP]

The camera is another person in the room.
Use intimate distance, natural perspective, and slightly imperfect framing.

Strong default photographic language:
- full-frame photographic feel
- 35-85mm equivalent depending on scene
- 4-7 foot camera distance for reference-grade body truth
- eye level or slightly low for mutual intimacy / confidence
- mild handheld imperfection
- shallow but not identity-destroying depth of field

PHOTOREALISM

Photorealism is non-negotiable unless another style is explicitly requested.

Preserve:
- pores
- peach fuzz / fine body hair
- tiny blemishes
- natural under-eye detail
- lip texture
- realistic eye moisture and sclera
- individual hair strands and flyaways
- believable skin compression where clothing meets the body
- realistic fabric tension
- natural folds
- imperfect symmetry
- authentic low-light noise where appropriate
- plausible lens depth and focus falloff
- physically coherent shadows and highlights

The result must read as a real photograph first, not AI art, CGI, glamour retouching, a fashion ad, or plastic influencer photography.

LIGHTING

[LIGHTING]

- warm practical key
- cooler environmental edge or window fill
- little flat frontal fill
- eyes still readable
- enough shadow for depth
- narrow highlights on lips, eyes, jewelry, damp skin, lace, satin, or glass
